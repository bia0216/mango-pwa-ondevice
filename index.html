      Disease scan

      Tips for a good photo
      • Show the spots: turn the mango so the dark patch or the stem end faces the camera.
      • Close and sharp: the mango or leaf fills most of the photo.
      • Leaf: one leaf on its own, no mangoes in the photo.
      • Light: daylight, no flash glare on the skin.

      Disease (our model)

      Good to know

      • Mango: healthy, anthracnose, stem-end rot (black tip once our own photos are added).
      • Leaf: healthy, bacterial canker, or another leaf disease. Photograph a leaf on its own: when mangoes are in the photo, only the mangoes are checked.
      • Several mangoes? Each gets its own disease result and heat map; tap a number to see its details.
      • Three disease models are compared on the same photos. Heat maps come from MobileNetV2 and EfficientNetV2-B0; YOLO26n-cls shows no heat map.
      • "Not a mango or leaf": no mango was found and our ripeness model also says it is not a mango. A rough check, not tested on many photos.
      • Verdict = our team's rule: disease ≥ 50% → Disease found; healthy ≥ 60% → Healthy; otherwise Check by hand. Not an official standard.
      • Ripeness is a separate scan in the Ripeness tab.
